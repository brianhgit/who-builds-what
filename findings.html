How sure are we? The 95% range vs. our 3% goal

The dot is our result. The bar is the range where 95% of 2,000 re-draws landed. It sits fully above zero (so the win is real), but it never reaches the 3% goal line (so it's not an official pass).

What does "missed by 3.44 points" mean?

A move of 4 means the stock moved about 4% more (or less) than the whole market over those 2 days. If the real move was 7 and we guessed 4, we missed by 3 points. Average that miss over every report and you get the number above. Lower is better.

Why 1,564 filings were dropped

How do we know the data is right?

Hand check (everyone): we compared our report dates against company press releases. With the 2-day window, dates we found were right, and we found reports we were supposed to find. That's why the team picked the 2-day window on Oct 26.

Timing check: our earnings move was bigger than the next normal day's move about of the time (depending on the year), and typically bigger. So we're catching the real reaction.

Price spot check (Daniel): moves matched Nasdaq. The one gap (Carrier) was a $0.15 dividend, which our prices adjust for on purpose.

Rebuilds: Charvee, Isani and Philip each rebuilt the table on their own computers and got exactly 14,491 kept and 1,564 dropped.

What's the "2-day earnings-window move"?

We take the stock's closing price the trading day before the report and the day after, and subtract what the whole market (SPY) did over the same days. Up or down doesn't matter, only the size. Two days catches the reaction whether the news came out before the open, during the day, or after the close.

7 · How to say itAccurate and positive

Quick word list

Simple guess (baseline): the median of a company's last 8 earnings moves.

Average miss (MAE): how far off a guess was, averaged over every report. Lower is better.

Practice years: 2022 to 2024. We trained on the past and tested one quarter at a time, moving forward.

Final exam (lockbox): Jan 2025 to Sep 2026, locked away and opened exactly once on Oct 31.

3% goal: set and locked on Oct 27, before any results existed.

LightGBM: the kind of model we used. It builds many small decision trees, each fixing the last one's mistakes.
